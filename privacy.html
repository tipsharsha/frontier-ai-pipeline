Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Frontier AI Pipeline ("the app") is a personal automation tool operated by a single YouTube creator for their own channel. It is not available to the public.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube Data API: to upload the operator's own videos, captions and thumbnails to the operator's own channel as private drafts, and to read comments on those videos.
  • YouTube Analytics API: to read view counts, watch time and audience-retention reports for the operator's own videos.

How data is used and stored

  • Analytics are used only to improve future videos, and aggregated reports are kept in the operator's private code repository.
  • OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the operator's private GitHub repository and are used only by its automated jobs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about viewers is collected beyond the aggregate statistics YouTube provides to channel owners.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
